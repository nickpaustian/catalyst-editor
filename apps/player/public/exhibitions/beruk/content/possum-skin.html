Possum skin, Dancer skirt

2018

Mandy Nicholson

Wurundjeri

Possum skin, waxed string, pyrography

Courtesy of the maker

The possum skirts are made and worn by each dancer, their totem and stories burnt into the skin side. These are worn for dance and ceremony; they can also be wrapped around the shoulders. The design burnt into this skirt is the Djeri, the grub, to honour Beruk and the Wurundjeri people.
