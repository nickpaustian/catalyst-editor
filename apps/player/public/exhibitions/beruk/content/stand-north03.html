In the image here, Beruk is holding up Lyrebird feathers.

The Lyrebird, a sacred spirit protector of the Wurundjeri – if you look carefully, you will see these feathers in many of Beruk’s ceremonial paintings. Traditionally used as a headdress on both men and women, these captivating feathers were worn in ceremonial dances called ‘Ngargee’. For the Wurundjeri, ceremonies would bring people together for a variety of important moments, such as family coming together to celebrate or mourn, for tribal relationships, spiritual responsibilities among many more.
