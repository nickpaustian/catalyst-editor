ʻOne of a set of 3 woven shields, created as a way to protect myself
ngulla ba mungeit, ngulla ba nuringian
marnangik berren berren, durndurnik wonthaggarook
ngalimbe ba mungeit, ngulla ba nuringian
narrkwarrenik mangmangnhan
ngulla ba mungeit, ngulla ba nuringian
narrangout mundagat

Sit and make, sit and think
Hands busy, my mind quiet
Sit and make, sit and think
Thinking about my family
Sit and make, sit and think
Shields to protect me’
